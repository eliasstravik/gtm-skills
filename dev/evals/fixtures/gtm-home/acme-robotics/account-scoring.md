# Account Scoring

## Fit Inputs

- ICP label match and evidence quality.
- Number of facilities, dock throughput, and urgency of delay signals.
- Stack compatibility and implementation complexity.

## Score Bands

- 90-100: urgent multi-site fit with verified dock delay pain.
- 75-89: strong fit with credible operations signals.
- 50-74: possible fit with gaps or weak timing.
- 1-49: no-match, disqualified, or insufficient evidence.

## Review Flags

Mark review needed when the account lacks source evidence for operational pain
or when a disqualifier may apply.
