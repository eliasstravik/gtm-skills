# Lead Scoring

## Fit Inputs

- Persona label match and seniority.
- Ownership of dock, fulfillment, labor, or logistics KPIs.
- Account fit and timing evidence.

## Score Bands

- 90-100: confirmed operations owner at a strong-fit account.
- 75-89: strong persona fit with credible pain evidence.
- 50-74: relevant role but limited authority or timing evidence.
- 1-49: no-match, disqualified, or insufficient evidence.

## Review Flags

Mark review needed when title evidence is ambiguous or buying authority is
unclear.
