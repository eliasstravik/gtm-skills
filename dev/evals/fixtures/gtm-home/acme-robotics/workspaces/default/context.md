# Default GTM Workspace

## What this workspace is for

Outbound and account research for warehouse automation opportunities.

## Offering

Dock scheduling automation, inventory movement analytics, and robotics workflow
consulting.

## Market

United States logistics, warehousing, fulfillment, and distribution companies
with 100 to 2,000 employees.

## GTM motion

Outbound to operations and supply-chain leaders, supported by account
segmentation, persona segmentation, and prioritized follow-up.

## Target outcomes

Qualified meetings with companies showing warehouse throughput, labor
efficiency, or dock-delay pains.

## Messaging notes

Emphasize operational throughput, fewer manual handoffs, and measurable dock
turnaround improvements.

## Constraints / disqualifiers

Avoid consumer ecommerce sellers with no owned warehouse operations and
enterprise-only robotics manufacturers outside the warehouse workflow.

## Notes / open questions

Budget and implementation timeline thresholds are not known.
