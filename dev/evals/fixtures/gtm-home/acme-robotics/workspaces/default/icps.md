# ICPs

## Segment: Mid-Market Warehouse Operators

Machine label: `mid-market-warehouse-operators`

Best-fit accounts operate warehouses, fulfillment centers, or distribution
networks with visible pressure to improve throughput, staffing efficiency, or
dock scheduling.

Strong signals:

- Owns or manages multiple warehouse or fulfillment sites.
- Mentions dock delays, labor shortages, throughput targets, or inventory
  movement visibility.
- Uses a warehouse management system or transportation management system.

Disqualifiers:

- No owned or operated warehouse workflow.
- Consumer-only seller with outsourced fulfillment and no operations team.
- Robotics manufacturer seeking product partnerships rather than workflow
  automation.

Confidence: high
Needs review: false

## Segment: Logistics Tech Integrators

Machine label: `logistics-tech-integrators`

Best-fit accounts provide logistics software, consulting, or systems
integration for warehouse operators and may partner on implementation.

Strong signals:

- Offers WMS, TMS, supply-chain analytics, or warehouse consulting.
- Serves mid-market logistics and fulfillment teams.
- Has partner or implementation services.

Disqualifiers:

- Pure freight brokerage with no warehouse operations or implementation scope.
- General IT consultancy with no logistics focus.

Confidence: medium
Needs review: true

## No Match

Machine label: `no-match`

Use `no-match` when an account lacks warehouse, fulfillment, logistics,
distribution, or implementation relevance.
