# Personas

## Persona: Warehouse Operations Leader

Machine label: `warehouse-operations-leader`

Relevant titles:

- VP Operations
- Director of Warehouse Operations
- Fulfillment Operations Manager
- Supply Chain Operations Director

Responsibilities:

- Owns throughput, staffing efficiency, dock scheduling, inventory movement,
  and operational process improvements.

Pain / priority signals:

- Dock delays, manual coordination, overtime pressure, slow inventory movement,
  or lack of real-time warehouse visibility.

Buying influence:

- Primary champion or economic buyer for warehouse workflow automation.

Disqualifiers:

- Role is purely corporate strategy with no operational ownership.

Confidence: high
Needs review: false

## Persona: Logistics Technology Owner

Machine label: `logistics-technology-owner`

Relevant titles:

- Director of IT
- Head of Supply Chain Systems
- WMS Administrator
- Logistics Systems Lead

Responsibilities:

- Owns warehouse systems, integrations, operational analytics, and technology
  rollout.

Pain / priority signals:

- Mentions WMS integrations, reporting gaps, operational data quality, or
  implementation risk.

Buying influence:

- Technical evaluator or implementation owner.

Disqualifiers:

- No ownership of warehouse systems or integrations.

Confidence: medium
Needs review: true

## No Match

Machine label: `no-match`

Use `no-match` when a lead does not match a defined persona or sits outside the
warehouse operations buying committee.
