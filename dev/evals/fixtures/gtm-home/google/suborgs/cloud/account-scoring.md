# Account Scoring

## Fit Inputs

- Cloud modernization mandate.
- Platform complexity and migration urgency.
- Executive sponsorship and security/compliance drivers.

## Score Bands

- 90-100: active cloud modernization with board-level urgency.
- 75-89: strong modernization signals and clear platform owner.
- 50-74: possible fit with weak timing.
- 1-49: no-match or no transformation evidence.
