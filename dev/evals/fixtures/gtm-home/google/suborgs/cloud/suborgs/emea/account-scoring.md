# Account Scoring

## Fit Inputs

- EMEA-specific mandate and regional owner.
- Data residency or regulatory urgency.
- Evidence that the active initiative is not global-only.

## Score Bands

- 90-100: urgent EMEA migration with regional executive ownership.
- 75-89: strong regional signals and credible timing.
- 50-74: relevant account with unclear regional mandate.
- 1-49: no-match or no EMEA evidence.
