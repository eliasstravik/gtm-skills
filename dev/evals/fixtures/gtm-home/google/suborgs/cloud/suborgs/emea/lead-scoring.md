# Lead Scoring

## Fit Inputs

- Persona label fit and regional remit.
- Buying influence over EMEA cloud platform decisions.
- Account fit and evidence quality.

## Score Bands

- 90-100: confirmed regional platform owner at a strong-fit account.
- 75-89: strong persona fit with credible regional mandate.
- 50-74: relevant title but weak authority or timing evidence.
- 1-49: no-match or insufficient evidence.
