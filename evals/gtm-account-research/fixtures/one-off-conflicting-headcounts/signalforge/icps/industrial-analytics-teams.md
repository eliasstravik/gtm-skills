# Industrial Analytics Teams

## Identity

- Qualified label: industrial-analytics-teams

## Account Profile

- Manufacturers with 500–5,000 employees and at least three sites.

## Fit Signals

- Internal operations analytics.
- Cloud data warehouse containing plant data.
- Active multi-site performance standardization.

## Buying Context

- Operations leaders need comparable plant evidence across sites.

## Disqualifiers

- Single-site operator.
- Fully outsourced analytics.
