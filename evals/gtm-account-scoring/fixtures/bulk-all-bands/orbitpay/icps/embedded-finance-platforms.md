# Embedded Finance Platforms

## Identity

- Qualified label: `embedded-finance-platforms`
- Status: `working definition`

## Account Profile

B2B software platforms that own an embedded accounts or payments product offered through a direct API.

## Fit Signals

- **Owned embedded product:** The account owns an embedded accounts or payments product.
- **Direct API:** The owned product is delivered through a direct customer API.
- **Dedicated risk ownership:** A dedicated internal risk team owns the product's investigations.
- **Active European launch:** A European market launch is active rather than merely planned.

## Buying Context

- Product expansion creates new fraud-case ownership requirements.

## Disqualifiers

- **Reseller-only model:** The account only resells third-party payment tools.
- **No owned product:** The account owns no embedded-finance product.

## Evidence And Confidence

- Confidence: medium.
- Source: five platform discovery programs.
