# Regional Digital Banks

## Identity

- Qualified label: `regional-digital-banks`
- Status: `working definition`

## Account Profile

Licensed digital banks operating in two or more European markets with at least 15 internal fraud analysts.

## Fit Signals

- **Operating-license fit:** The account holds a direct banking operating license.
- **Internal fraud ownership:** An internal fraud operations team owns investigations.
- **Migration backlog urgency:** A monitoring-system migration has created a case backlog.

## Buying Context

- Fraud leaders need to stabilize case ownership after monitoring changes.

## Disqualifiers

- **Outsourced investigation ownership:** An external provider owns investigation decisions or case resolution.
- **No operating license:** The account lacks a direct banking operating license.

## Evidence And Confidence

- Confidence: high.
- Source: seven qualified bank opportunities.

## Review Needs

- Recheck the analyst threshold after two smaller-bank pilots.

## Open Questions

- Whether single-market banks need a separate motion.
