# Account Scoring

Add the four components; maximum 100.

## Components
- Segment evidence: high 40; medium 28; low 12; `no-match` 0.
- Qualification coverage: complete 25; partial 12; unknown 0.
- Timing: active 20; emerging 10; none 0.
- Evidence quality: corroborated 15; single supplied source 8; unknown 0.

## Bands
- 0–39: no-fit
- 40–64: nurture
- 65–84: priority
- 85–100: top-priority

## Guardrail
- `no-match` is capped at 39.
