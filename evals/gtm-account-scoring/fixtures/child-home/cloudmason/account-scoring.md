# Global Account Scoring

## Components
- Segment evidence: high 50; medium 30; low 10; `no-match` 0.
- Platform maturity: complete 25; partial 12; unknown 0.
- Timing: active 15; emerging 7; none 0.
- Evidence quality: corroborated 10; supplied 5; unknown 0.

## Bands
- 0–39: no-fit
- 40–69: develop
- 70–89: priority
- 90–100: strategic
