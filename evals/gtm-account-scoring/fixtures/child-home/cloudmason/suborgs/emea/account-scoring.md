# EMEA Account Scoring

Add the four components; maximum 100.

## Components
- Segment evidence: high 30; medium 20; low 8; `no-match` 0.
- Regulatory readiness: complete 30; partial 15; unknown 0.
- Program timing: active 25; emerging 12; none 0.
- Evidence quality: corroborated 15; single supplied source 8; unknown 0.

## Bands
- 0–39: no-fit
- 40–64: monitor
- 65–84: priority
- 85–100: immediate
