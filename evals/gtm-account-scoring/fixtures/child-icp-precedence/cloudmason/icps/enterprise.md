# Global Enterprise Software Companies

## Identity

- Qualified label: `enterprise`

## Account Profile

Global B2B software companies with more than 2,000 employees and cloud teams in at least three continents.

## Fit Signals

- **Central platform ownership:** A central cloud-platform organization owns the program.
- **Global modernization program:** A multi-region modernization program is active.
- **Developer-tooling mandate:** Developer tooling is a named enterprise priority.
- **Regional controls motion:** Regional controls are part of the global program.

## Disqualifiers
