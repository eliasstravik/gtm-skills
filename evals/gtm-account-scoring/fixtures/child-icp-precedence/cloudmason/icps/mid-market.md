# Mid-Market Software Companies

## Identity

- Qualified label: `mid-market`
- Status: `working definition`

## Account Profile

European B2B software companies with 100–999 employees and one central cloud-platform team.

## Fit Signals

- **Small platform ownership:** One internal platform team owns the cloud estate.
- **First governance initiative:** The account is starting its first centralized governance initiative.

## Buying Context

- Growth creates inconsistent controls across product teams.

## Disqualifiers

- **Above mid-market scale:** The account has more than 999 employees.
- **Regulated financial institution:** The account is a regulated financial institution.

## Evidence And Confidence

- Confidence: medium.
- Source: 2025 mid-market review.
