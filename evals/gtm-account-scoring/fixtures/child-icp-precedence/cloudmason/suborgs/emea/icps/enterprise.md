# EMEA Regulated Enterprises

## Identity

- Qualified label: `emea/enterprise`
- Status: `working definition`

## Account Profile

EEA-regulated financial institutions with 800–5,000 employees and operations in at least two EEA countries.

## Fit Signals

- **Dedicated controls ownership:** A dedicated cloud-controls team owns the program.
- **DORA remediation urgency:** An active DORA remediation program requires reviewable evidence.
- **Multi-country EEA operations:** The account operates in at least two EEA countries.

## Buying Context

- Regulatory remediation requires reviewable control evidence.

## Disqualifiers

- **No EEA-regulated entity:** The account has no EEA-regulated operating entity.
- **No controls owner:** The account has no internal cloud-controls owner.

## Evidence And Confidence

- Confidence: medium.
- Source: six EMEA discovery programs.
