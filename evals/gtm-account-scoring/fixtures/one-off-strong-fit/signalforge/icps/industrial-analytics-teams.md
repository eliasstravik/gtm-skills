# Industrial Analytics Teams

## Identity

- Qualified label: `industrial-analytics-teams`
- Status: `working definition`

## Account Profile

Industrial manufacturers with 500–5,000 employees and at least two owned production sites in Northern Europe.

## Fit Signals

- **Internal analytics ownership:** An internal operations-analytics team owns plant-performance work.
- **Cloud plant-data foundation:** A cloud data warehouse contains plant data.
- **Multi-site standardization urgency:** An active program is standardizing performance across multiple sites.

## Buying Context

- Leadership needs consistent plant-performance evidence across countries.

## Disqualifiers

- **No owned production:** The account owns no production operations.
- **No internal owner:** A single-site business lacks an internal analytics owner.

## Evidence And Confidence

- Confidence: medium.
- Source: five won opportunities reviewed in 2026.

- Test whether Snowflake specifically remains a useful warehouse proxy.
