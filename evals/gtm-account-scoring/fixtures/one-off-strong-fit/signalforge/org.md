# Signalforge

## Background

Signalforge sells plant-performance analytics software to industrial operators in Northern Europe.

## Offers

- Plant-performance analytics subscription.
- Data-warehouse and production-system implementation.

## Positioning

Give multi-site operations teams one evidence base for performance standardization.

## ICP And Persona Notes

The primary motion targets industrial operators with owned sites and internal analytics capability. Logistics software companies are a distinct secondary motion.

## Constraints

- Never assume an account owns facilities unless supplied evidence says so.
- Do not treat an ICP document's maintenance work as missing account evidence.
