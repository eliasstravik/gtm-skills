# OrbitPay

## Background

OrbitPay sells fraud-case workflow software to digital banks and embedded-finance platforms in Europe.

## Offers

- Fraud operations workflow subscription.
- Monitoring-migration and API implementation.

## Positioning

Help licensed financial operators and embedded product teams manage fraud cases without losing ownership.

## ICP And Persona Notes

Digital banks and embedded-finance platforms use separate qualification motions.

## Constraints

- Do not infer licenses, internal teams, products, or markets from a company category alone.
- Unlicensed consultants and tool resellers are not operators for either motion.
