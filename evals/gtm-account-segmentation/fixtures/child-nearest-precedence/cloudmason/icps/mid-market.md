# Mid-Market Software Companies

## Identity

- Qualified label: `mid-market`
- Status: `working definition`

## Account Profile

European B2B software companies with 100–999 employees and one central cloud-platform team.

## Fit Signals

- Cloud estate spans at least two providers.
- Platform ownership is internal.

## Buying Context

- Growth creates inconsistent controls across product teams.

## Disqualifiers

- Fewer than 100 employees.
- More than 999 employees.

## Evidence And Confidence

- Confidence: medium.
- Source: 2025 mid-market review.

## Review Needs

- Validate the lower employee boundary.

## Open Questions

- Whether developer-tool companies need a separate motion.
