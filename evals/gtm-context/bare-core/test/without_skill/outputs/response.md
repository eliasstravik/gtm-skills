You don’t need to be technical to get this started. The first goal isn’t to build a complicated “GTM system”; it’s to turn the material you already have into one clear, trustworthy place your team can use.

Start with three small steps:

1. **Protect the originals.** Make a backup copy of the folder before reorganizing anything.
2. **Take inventory.** List the files and note what each contains—company strategy, product notes, customer research, target accounts, messaging, teammate roles, or something else. Don’t worry about tidying yet.
3. **Create a one-page GTM brief.** Pull together the answers you already have, and mark the rest as unknown:
   - What do we sell?
   - Who is it for?
   - What problem do we solve?
   - Why would someone choose us?
   - How do we reach potential customers?
   - Who on the team owns each part?

A simple eventual folder structure could look like this:

```text
GTM Workspace/
  00 Start Here
  Company and Product
  Customers and ICP
  Personas
  Messaging
  Accounts and Leads
  Campaigns and Channels
  Team and Ownership
  Research
  Archive
```

Keep personal teammate information to what is actually useful for collaboration—roles, responsibilities, expertise, and preferred contact method—and avoid copying sensitive personal data into a shared workspace.
