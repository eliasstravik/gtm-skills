# HelioDesk

## Background

HelioDesk sells customer-support workflow software to European service teams.
The company has a general platform and a regulated-industry product line.

## Offers

- Customer-support workflow platform.
- Analytics and knowledge-management add-ons.

## Positioning

Give support leaders one operating view across channels and teams.

## ICP And Persona Notes

Company-wide targeting focuses on multi-team support organizations. Regulated
buyers require a distinct product and qualification motion.

## Constraints

- Do not make product-line-specific claims at company level.
- The general platform is not positioned as a compliance system.
