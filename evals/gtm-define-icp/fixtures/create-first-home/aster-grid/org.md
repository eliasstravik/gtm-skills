# Aster Grid

## Background

Aster Grid sells outage-planning software to electricity distribution
operators in Northern Europe. The platform combines asset history, weather
exposure, and crew availability for maintenance planning.

## Offers

- Annual software subscription for outage and maintenance planning.
- Paid implementation for asset-data integration.

## Positioning

Replace spreadsheet-based outage planning with one auditable operating model.

## ICP And Persona Notes

The current hypothesis favors distribution operators with internal field crews
and aging grid assets. No detailed ICP has been approved yet.

## Constraints

- Do not claim the product predicts extreme-weather events.
- Transmission system operators have not been validated as buyers.
