# Regional Carriers

## Identity

- Qualified label: `regional-carriers`
- Status: `working definition`

## Account Profile

European road-freight carriers operating 50–500 vehicles across multiple
depots.

## Fit Signals

- Dedicated fleet-maintenance or compliance team.
- Cross-border operations in at least two markets.

## Buying Context

- Audit preparation exposes missing or inconsistent vehicle records.
- Growth through depot acquisition creates disconnected workflows.

## Disqualifiers

- Passenger-only fleets.

## Evidence And Confidence

- Source: founder and sales-team discovery notes.
