# Caregrid

## Background

Caregrid sells casework coordination software to health and public-service organizations. The company has a general care-navigation platform and a dedicated public-sector product line.

## Offers

- Care-navigation workflow platform.
- Reporting and integration add-ons.

## Positioning

Give service teams consistent ownership across complex cases.

## ICP And Persona Notes

Company-wide targeting focuses on multi-site health systems. Government benefits programs use a separate product and buying motion.

## Constraints

- Do not make statutory-service claims at company level.
- The general platform is not positioned as a public-benefits eligibility system.
