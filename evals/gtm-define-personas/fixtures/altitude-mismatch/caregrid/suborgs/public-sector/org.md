# Caregrid Public Sector

## Background

The public-sector product line provides controlled queue changes and audit trails for regional benefits casework.

## Offers

- Caseworker Enablement for government benefits agencies.

## Positioning

Keep case ownership visible while agencies change queues and service rules.

## ICP And Persona Notes

Government benefits agencies are the validated account focus. Program operations directors own service standards across regional teams.

## Constraints

- Never claim that Caregrid determines benefit eligibility.
- Central IT approval remains necessary for technical changes.
