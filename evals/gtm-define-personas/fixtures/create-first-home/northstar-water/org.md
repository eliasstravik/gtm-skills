# Northstar Water

## Background

Northstar Water sells leak-response coordination software to municipal water
utilities in Northern Europe.

## Offers

- Annual Leak Response Orchestration subscription.
- Implementation for GIS and work-order integration.

## Positioning

Coordinate network incidents from detection through field resolution without
relying on shared spreadsheets and phone trees.

## Constraints

- Do not claim the product detects leaks itself.
- Procurement consultants and student researchers are not buying contacts.
