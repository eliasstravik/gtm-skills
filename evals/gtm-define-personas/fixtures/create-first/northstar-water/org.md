# Northstar Water

## Background

Northstar Water sells incident-coordination software to municipal water utilities in the United Kingdom and Ireland. The product links detection events, network control, and field response without replacing systems of record.

## Offers

- Annual workflow subscription for leak and interruption response.
- Implementation for GIS and work-order integrations.

## Positioning

Make every operational handoff visible while existing systems remain authoritative.

## ICP And Persona Notes

Municipal water utilities are the current account focus. Buyer research has concentrated on network operations leadership.

## Constraints

- Never claim that Northstar Water replaces a GIS or work-order system.
- Procurement advisers and academic researchers are not operating owners.
