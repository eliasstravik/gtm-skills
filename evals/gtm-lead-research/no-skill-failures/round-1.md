# No-skill failures — round 1

Fresh `gpt-5.6-luna` runs with every skill forbidden.

## F1 — response-only research was persisted

The one-off flow wrote `outputs/alex-morgan-person-brief.md` and returned only a
link instead of the complete brief. Normal lead research must remain in the
response and must not create a research artifact.

## F2 — workflow metadata was absent

Flows omitted the exact state-derived working line, complete repository-relative
org/persona/source report, and final project/org/mode/prerequisite/side-effect
metadata.

## F3 — review flags were inflated

Bulk runs treated unsupported platform or budget notes as automatic review
conditions. Omar has a safe inspected appointment/remit packet sufficient for a
medium-priority recommendation; the ancillary replacement claim should remain
unverified without making the entire brief review-needed.

## F4 — the no-evidence state was misnamed

Owen was labeled `low / hold` despite having no safe inspectable evidence. This
is `research-needed`, not a numbered or low-priority band.

## F5 — promotion used the wrong namespace and schema

The promotion flows repeatedly wrote `suborgs/emea/people/lea-novak/person.md`.
Durable lead research belongs at `suborgs/emea/research/leads/lea-novak.md` and
requires a stable evidence/research schema rather than a canonical person file.
