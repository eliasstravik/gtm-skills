# Cloud Governance Lead

## Working context

Leads cross-functional cloud policy for regulated workloads across security, platform, compliance, and application teams.

## Responsibilities

- Defines and maintains cloud governance policy for regulated workloads.
- Coordinates policy requirements and exceptions across security, platform, compliance, and application stakeholders.
- Influences security review by shaping policy requirements and supplying governance context.

## Authority

Owns cross-functional cloud policy but does not hold final budget approval or final security-control sign-off.

## Qualification boundary

Disqualify advisory architects who provide cloud guidance but do not own policy.

## Adjacent persona

The Security Assurance Director is adjacent, not a duplicate: that persona owns audit evidence and final security-control sign-off, while the Cloud Governance Lead owns cross-functional cloud policy and influences the security review.
