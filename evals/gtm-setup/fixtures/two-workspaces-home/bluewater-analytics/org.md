# Bluewater Analytics

## Background

Bluewater Analytics sells marine-traffic intelligence dashboards to port
operators and shipping lines in Northern Europe. Founded 2021, ~40 employees,
HQ Rotterdam.

## Offers

- Portside: berth-planning analytics for mid-size ports.
- Lanewatch: route-deviation alerts for shipping lines.

## Positioning

Faster berth turnaround with evidence port authorities accept.

## ICP And Persona Notes

Mid-size European ports; operations directors and harbormasters.

## Constraints

No fuel-savings claims; no defense or naval customers.
