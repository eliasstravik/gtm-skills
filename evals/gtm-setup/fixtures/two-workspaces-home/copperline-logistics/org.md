# Copperline Logistics

## Background

Copperline Logistics is a mid-market 3PL offering contract warehousing and
cross-border freight across the Benelux and DACH regions. ~350 employees,
HQ Antwerp.

## Offers

- Contract warehousing (three owned sites).
- Cross-border FTL/LTL freight brokerage.

## Positioning

One accountable partner from dock to door across Benelux–DACH lanes.

## ICP And Persona Notes

Mid-market manufacturers shipping 20+ cross-border loads per month.

## Constraints

No cold-chain claims; no ocean freight.
