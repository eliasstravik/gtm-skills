# Copperline Freight

## Background

The freight brokerage division: cross-border FTL and LTL between Benelux and
DACH, sold separately from warehousing.

## Offers

- Spot and contract FTL.
- Consolidated LTL lanes Antwerp–Cologne–Munich.

## Positioning

Guaranteed capacity on Benelux–DACH lanes without asset-owner lock-in.

## ICP And Persona Notes

Logistics managers at manufacturers with weekly cross-border volume.

## Constraints

No dangerous-goods (ADR) claims.
