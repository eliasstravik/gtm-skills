# Northstar Compliance

## What this organization is

Northstar Compliance is a fictional B2B SaaS company selling an AI-assisted compliance operations workspace. The product helps compliance, operations, and risk teams coordinate onboarding reviews, evidence collection, policy checks, exception queues, and audit-ready handoffs.

This fixture is intentionally fictional and is safe for examples, tests, and skill evals.

## Products / offerings

- Northstar Compliance Workspace: AI-assisted task intake, compliance queue triage, reviewer notes, policy checklist generation, and audit trail summaries.
- Compliance Operations Starter: outbound-friendly package for teams with manual KYC, KYB, vendor, or customer onboarding review queues.

## Positioning

Northstar helps regulated teams move compliance work out of scattered tickets, spreadsheets, and chat threads into one governed operating workspace. The approved message is operational relief: faster review cycles, clearer ownership, and better audit readiness.

## Proof points

Fixture-only proof points for examples:

- Fictional users report fewer duplicate compliance reviews after centralizing evidence and reviewer notes.
- Fictional operations teams use Northstar to standardize policy checklists across onboarding queues.
- Fictional risk teams use Northstar summaries to prepare audit handoffs faster.

## Constraints / things to avoid

- Do not claim Northstar replaces legal counsel, compliance officers, auditors, or required regulatory judgment.
- Do not claim automated approval of regulated customers, merchants, vendors, or financial accounts.
- Do not position the product as fraud detection, transaction monitoring, sanctions screening, or identity verification infrastructure.
- Avoid examples that imply real customers, live integrations, or private data.
