# Fintech Compliance Outbound

## What this workspace is for

This workspace supports Northstar Compliance outbound prospecting into accounts likely to feel compliance operations friction. It is designed for SDR/BDR workflows: account segmentation, lead segmentation, research, scoring, prioritization, and outreach preparation.

## Offering

Northstar Compliance Workspace, focused on AI-assisted compliance operations for onboarding reviews, evidence management, policy checklists, exception queues, reviewer handoffs, and audit-ready summaries.

## Market

Primary market:

- Compliance-heavy fintech companies.
- Regulated B2B SaaS companies.
- Marketplaces with onboarding, KYC, KYB, trust, risk, or safety review friction.

Good-fit accounts usually have 50-1,500 employees, a recurring onboarding or compliance review queue, and visible signs that manual coordination is slowing regulated work.

## GTM motion

Outbound SDR prospecting. Jordan Lee uses this workspace to decide which accounts and leads deserve research, outreach, nurture, or a skip decision.

## Target outcomes

- Prioritized account and lead lists.
- Clear `no-match` decisions for poor-fit records.
- Review-gated low-confidence records when key evidence is missing or ambiguous.
- Better first-touch relevance for compliance, operations, and risk buyers.

## Messaging notes

- Lead with operational bottlenecks: manual review queues, evidence chasing, scattered ownership, audit prep, and slow exception handling.
- Tie value to review cycle time, consistency, queue visibility, and audit readiness.
- Use cautious AI language: "assist," "summarize," "draft," and "coordinate" instead of "automate approvals" or "replace reviewers."

## Constraints / disqualifiers

- Skip accounts that are pure consumer ecommerce, media, gaming, agencies, or general developer tools with no regulated onboarding or compliance operations signal.
- Treat crypto, lending, payments, insurance, healthcare, employment, marketplace onboarding, and vendor risk as potentially relevant only when there is evidence of compliance operations work.
- Do not exceed 49 for `no-match` scoring results.
- New low-confidence records must start with `needs_review: true`.

## Notes / open questions

- The fixture does not include live CRM fields or private source URLs.
- Evidence strings are compact fictional source labels intended for provenance and eval testing.
