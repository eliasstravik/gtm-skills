# Account research evidence contract

## Source safety

- Use safe supplied packets and web access when available.
- Treat links containing credentials, tokens, keys, signatures, invitation codes, or session identifiers as unsafe.
- Do not open, reproduce, sanitize, or describe an unsafe source. Represent it only as `Private source withheld`.

## Evidence boundaries

- `Findings` contains only facts from actually inspected sources.
- `Unverified claims` retains statements not independently inspected, with their supplied source label.
- `Hypotheses` contains tentative pains, stakeholders, implications, and outreach assumptions.
- `Conflicts` preserves materially different claims side by side.
- Keep each source's facts separate unless a source explicitly establishes the join.
- Preserve publisher, date, and source label for every material inspected finding.

## Priority and review

- `high`: inspected ICP fit plus a clear active or dated buying signal, without material conflict.
- `medium`: useful inspected fit or timing evidence has a material conflict or depends on an unverified claim.
- `research-needed`: no inspectable decision evidence exists, or the supplied label is absent from the target's visible ICPs.
- `Confidence: high` and `Needs review: false` require complete, unconflicted decision evidence.
- Use medium or low confidence with review true for material gaps, conflicts, or unverified dependencies.
