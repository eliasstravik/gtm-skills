# Account research output

For one account, render this exact section order:

```markdown
# <Account> account brief

## Executive brief
<identity, supplied label unchanged, concise disposition>

## Findings
<inspected facts with publisher, date, and source>

## Unverified claims
<claims not inspected, or None evidenced>

## Hypotheses
<tentative pains or stakeholders, or None evidenced>

## Fit
<state "Label preserved without re-segmentation," then compare with the supplied label's visible ICP prose>

## Timing
<dated or active signals and gaps>

## Risks
<disqualifiers, conflicts, gaps, and unknowns>

## Personalization angles
<evidence-grounded themes, not invented facts>

## Recommended next step
<one concrete action>

## Priority
Priority: <high|medium|research-needed>
Confidence: <high|medium|low>
Needs review: <true|false>

## Sources
<safe source labels with publisher/date; unsafe source only as Private source withheld>
```

For bulk work, open with:

```markdown
Research-priority distribution:
- high: <count>
- medium: <count>
- research-needed: <count>
```

Then repeat the complete brief for each account. For a save request, first say `Under the org-only principle, account research is not stored in the GTM context repo.` Then render the same complete brief as copyable Markdown. End every completed or prerequisite-stop response exactly:

`No files, git history, or external systems changed.`
