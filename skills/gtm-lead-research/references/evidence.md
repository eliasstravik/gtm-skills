# Lead research evidence contract

- Use safe supplied packets and web access when available.
- Treat credentialed or tokenized links as unsafe; do not open, reproduce, sanitize, or describe them. Use only `Private source withheld`.
- `Findings` contains facts from inspected sources.
- `Unverified claims` retains statements not inspected with their supplied source label.
- `Role and influence hypotheses` and `Pain hypotheses` stay tentative.
- `Conflicts` preserves materially different claims side by side.
- Keep source facts separate unless a source explicitly establishes the join.
- Preserve publisher, date, and source label for every material finding.

Priority:

- `high`: inspected persona relevance plus a clear active or dated signal, without material conflict.
- `medium`: useful relevance or timing evidence has a material conflict or unverified dependency.
- `research-needed`: no inspectable decision evidence exists, or the supplied persona label is not visible.
- High confidence without review requires complete unconflicted evidence; gaps or conflicts lower confidence and require review.
