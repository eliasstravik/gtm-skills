# Lead research output

For each person, use:

```markdown
# <Person> person brief

## Executive brief
<person, company, title, supplied persona label unchanged>

## Findings
<inspected role facts with publisher, date, source>

## Unverified claims
<claims not inspected, or None evidenced>

## Role and influence hypotheses
<tentative interpretations, or None evidenced>

## Pain hypotheses
<tentative interpretations, or None evidenced>

## Persona relevance
Label preserved without re-segmentation. <comparison with visible persona prose>

## Timing
<dated signals and gaps>

## Risks
<conflicts, gaps, and unknowns>

## Outreach angles
<evidence-grounded themes>

## Recommended next step
<one concrete action>

## Priority
Priority: <high|medium|research-needed>
Confidence: <high|medium|low>
Needs review: <true|false>

## Sources
<safe labels with publisher/date; unsafe only as Private source withheld>
```

Bulk output begins:

```markdown
Research-priority distribution:
- high: <count>
- medium: <count>
- research-needed: <count>
```

Then repeat the complete brief per person. For a save request, first say `Under the org-only principle, lead research is not stored in the GTM context repo.` Then provide the full brief as copyable Markdown. End exactly:

`No files, git history, or external systems changed.`
